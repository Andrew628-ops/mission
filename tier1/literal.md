**Ingredients:** Bread, egg, cooking oil, water, milk, sugar and a tea bag

**Equipment** Plate rack, plates, fridge, kitchen cabinet, small mixing bowl, fork, frying pan, gas cooker, electric jug (kettle), cup, teaspoon

**Instructions Steps**

1. Go to the kitchen to prepare a breakfast of bread, fried egg, and a cup of tea.
2. Take a plate from the plate rack.
3. Take the bread from the fridge and place it on the plate.
4. Take a small mixing bowl from the kitchen cabinet.
5. Take an egg from the crate, crack it into the mixing bowl, and beat it thoroughly.
6. Pick up a frying pan and a bottle of cooking oil from the kitchen cabinet.
7. Turn on the gas cooker and place the frying pan on the burner.
8. Pour a little oil into the frying pan and allow it to heat up for 30 seconds.
9. Pour the beaten egg from the mixing bowl into the frying pan, flipping it every 15 seconds.
10. After 1 minute, remove the frying pan from the heat and turn off the gas.
11. Take another plate from the plate rack and transfer the fried egg from the frying pan onto the plate.
12. Pick up an electric jug, pour 1 liter of water into it, plug it into a socket, and switch it on to boil for 2 minutes.
13. Once boiled, switch off the socket, unplug the electric jug, take a cup from the cabinet, and pour the hot water into the cup (add your tea bag or tea leaves here if needed).
14. Pick up the milk tin from the shelf, scoop two teaspoons of milk into the cup, and stir for 15 seconds.
15. Pick up the sugar packet from the shelf, put 2 cubes of sugar into the cup, and stir for 10 seconds.
16. Serve the bread, fried egg, and cup of tea on the table.

**Question 1**
What is the computer doing?

**Answer:** The computer executes the program's instructions, processes the customer's data, stores the information in memory, and produces the final output.


**Question 2**
What is the program doing?

**Answer:** The program contains the instructions been executed

**Question 3**
What data is involved?

**Answer:** The data supplied by the customer (such as their name, order items, quantities, payment amount, and account status)

**Question 4**
What instructions are being executed?

**Answer:** the step by step command written in the program

**Question 5**
What is the output?

**Answer:** The final result of the instruction executed.

**Question 6**
What information might need to be stored for the system to remember the order?

**Answer:** Specific order details such as the Customer ID/Name, Order ID, items ordered, quantity, total price, date/time, and payment status.
